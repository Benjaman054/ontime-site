ON-Time

ON-Time — know exactly when to leave for every meeting.

ON-Time is an app that reads your Google Calendar, checks live traffic, and tells you the precise time to head out — so you're never late and never leave too early.

What ON-Time does

📅

Reads your calendar

Securely connects to your Google Calendar and finds your upcoming meetings that have a location.

🚗

Checks live traffic

Uses current driving conditions to work out how long the trip will really take today.

⏰

Tells you when to leave

Gives you a clear "leave by" time for each meeting, based on where you're starting from.

✉️

Daily summary

Optionally sends your plan for the day by email and Telegram, at a time you choose.

How it works

1

Sign in with Google

Connect your calendar in a couple of taps. Your credentials stay safe on our servers.

2

Set your home address & preferences

Tell ON-Time where you usually start from, when to notify you, and how far ahead to look.

3

Get your leave-by times

See traffic-aware departure times for every meeting — plus an optional daily summary.

What Google data ON-Time uses, and why

To provide its features, ON-Time requests access to your Google Calendar events (the calendar.events permission). We use this access for two things, and nothing else:

  • Reading your upcoming events — we read each event's title, time, and location so we can calculate a traffic-aware "leave by" time and send your optional daily summary.
  • Creating events you add in the app — when you add a meeting inside ON-Time, we write it to your Google Calendar.

We request the narrow calendar.events scope only. We do not access your calendar settings, sharing, or other calendars. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Your privacy comes first.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. Calendar data is used only to power the app's features.
